PRIVACY & COPPA NOTICE

NextMove Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 26, 2026

NextMove is a 13+ MVP for AI coaching across homework, gaming, and sports. This notice explains what the prototype collects, how it uses information, and the current teen-safety limits.

Important age limit

NextMove is available only for learners age 13 and older in this public MVP. Under-13 users may not create accounts, upload content, receive reports, or use coaching chat. Learners ages 13 to 17 must have parent or guardian permission to use NextMove.

If NextMove later supports under-13 users, it must add a verified parental consent process, parent access/deletion controls, and additional child-safety review before collecting personal information from children under 13.

Information NextMove may collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password hash, date of birth or age, and session cookies.
  • Homework uploads: images or PDFs submitted for coaching.
  • Gaming and sports uploads: sampled frames from short video clips.
  • Sports context: sport, position, and optional jersey number or jersey name/text if the user says those details are on their uniform. Athlete reference photos are not accepted in the public MVP.
  • Coaching content: notes, generated reports, and follow-up chat messages tied to a saved report.
  • Friends and party content: friend requests, accepted friend lists, party membership, shared report references, party chat messages, and safety reports.
  • Local reward data: coins and game unlocks stored in the browser for the current device.

How information is used

  • To create coaching reports and follow-up tutor responses.
  • To keep homework coaching focused on hints instead of final answers.
  • To keep sports and gaming coaching focused on visible performance, not identity or sensitive traits.
  • To manage accounts, sessions, reports, and coins.
  • To support accepted-friend chat, party collaboration, blocking/removal, and message reports.

Uploads and retention

Original uploads and sampled video frames are processed in memory for a report and are not written to NextMove storage. Generated reports and account records are retained in the app database until deleted by the user or reset by the operator.

AI and service providers

NextMove may send submitted content and coaching context to AI service providers to generate coaching. Hosting and email providers may process information needed to run the service and support account messages. These providers should be used only to provide the service and should be evaluated for privacy, security, and data-handling practices before production launch.

Sports clips, identity, and medical limits

NextMove is not for identifying a person by name or inferring sensitive traits from images or video. Sports coaching is not medical advice and should not be used to diagnose injuries or decide whether to play through pain.

Friends, parties, and reports

Friends and Party are intended only for people the user knows. Users should not share private details, passwords, addresses, school schedules, or sensitive information in chat. Safety reports may be stored so the operator can review misuse. Reported messages may be removed from the app database.

Parent rights and requests

NextMove does not support under-13 accounts in this public MVP. If an operator learns that an account belongs to a user under 13, the account and related data should be deleted. Parents or guardians may contact the operator to request deletion.

Data minimization

Users should only upload what is needed for coaching. Jersey details should be used only to help the coach distinguish the learner's player in a sports clip, not to identify a person in the real world. NextMove should not ask for a face photo, video, or extra personal details unless they are reasonably necessary for the selected coaching activity.
