Age requirement

NextMove is available only for students age 13 and older in this public MVP. Do not create an account or upload content if you are under 13. If you are 13 to 17, you may use NextMove only with permission from your parent or guardian. By creating an account, you confirm that you are 13 or older, have any required parent or guardian permission, and agree to these Terms and the Privacy Notice.

Coaching only

NextMove provides educational, gaming, and sports coaching suggestions. It is not a substitute for a teacher, coach, parent, doctor, lawyer, or other professional.

Homework integrity

Homework coaching is designed to give hints and explain steps. Users should not use NextMove to cheat, submit AI-generated work as their own, or bypass school rules.

Uploads

Only upload content you have the right to submit. Avoid uploading private, sensitive, or unnecessary personal information. Do not upload bystanders or other people unless the clip is necessary for coaching and you have the right to share it. Athlete reference photos are not accepted in the public MVP; for sports, use jersey number, jersey name/text, and visible clip context only to distinguish the learner's player.

Friends and party safety

Use Friends and Party only with people you know. Do not share private details, passwords, addresses, school schedules, or sensitive information in chat. Users can remove friends, block friends, leave parties, and report messages.

Sports safety

Sports coaching is not medical advice. NextMove does not diagnose injuries or recommend playing through pain. If something hurts or an injury may be involved, stop and ask a qualified adult, coach, clinician, or professional.

No identity or sensitive-trait use

NextMove is not for identifying people by name, verifying identity, or inferring sensitive traits from images or video.
